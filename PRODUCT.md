# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated by the approved build brief: plain ES modules, static HTML/CSS, Node's built-in test runner, deployable on Vercel without runtime secrets. This is a routine implementation choice under the user's instruction to progress autonomously.

## Users
An Espoo recruitment event organiser preparing a small, in-person introduction schedule. Companies and talent review and agree to introductions.

## Product purpose
Turn explicit company tasks and transferable work evidence into a proposed meeting schedule, then record both parties' agreement and issue a practical introduction card.

## Operating context
Synthetic event only. Scarce table slots, availability conflicts, mutual preferences, cancellation and reassignment. A judge should open the app, inspect why two parties might talk, record both demo confirmations, and see the table, time and conversation starter.

## Capabilities and constraints
No real applicant ingestion. No employment decision or rejection. Structured evidence tags are entered explicitly, never inferred from names, CV language, education or nationality. Human agreement is bound to the exact meeting. Local browser state only, no actual messages or invitations. Public MIT code and Vercel deployment required. Legal compliance has not been independently established.

## Evidence on hand
Official challenge text and C-14/C-16 brief. No real participant or employer studies, no human hiring labels, no observed conversion gains. Evaluation uses clearly labelled, constructed fixtures and programmatic constraints.

## Product principles
Show the concrete task and evidence together. End in an in-person introduction. Make exceptions and missing evidence visible. Keep numbers traceable to synthetic tests. People choose whether to meet.
